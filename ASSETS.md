# Asset policy

The visual assets in this repository support a demonstration build of **Freiburg–Konstanz — A Black
Forest cycling arcade.** They are not covered by the repository's MIT source-code license.

## Scope

Unless an individual file is accompanied by an explicit license notice, this policy applies to
visual and media files under:

- `src/assets/`
- `public/assets/`
- other image, audio, video, font, or artwork files committed to the repository

## Reuse and redistribution

The repository does not grant permission to reuse, modify, redistribute, sell, or sublicense these
assets. Before publishing or reusing an asset, verify:

1. its original source and creator;
2. the license or written permission that applies to it;
3. whether attribution, modification, or commercial-use restrictions apply;
4. whether depicted brands, people, locations, or other protected material require additional
   permission.

If provenance cannot be established, replace the asset with work that has clear, documented rights
before release.

## Adding assets

Every newly contributed asset should include its source, author or generator, license, permitted
uses, required attribution, and relevant modification notes in the pull request. A future asset
manifest may record this information per file; until then, absence of an entry must not be
interpreted as permission.
